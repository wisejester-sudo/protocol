Protocol

A private logbook for what you take, when you take it, and what your bloodwork does about it.

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no sign-up, no servers, nothing to leak.

What it does

Schedules that actually fitEvery-other-day, fixed weekdays, several doses a day, on/off cycles, titration ramps, start and end dates. It previews your next five doses before you save.
Reminders that hold upTaken and Snooze straight from the lock screen, with an honest read on how far ahead you're covered.
Reconstitution, drawn to scaleEnter the vial contents and diluent; see the fill line on a to-scale U-100 syringe, plus how many doses the vial holds.
Bloodwork that means somethingPaste your results and confirm what it found. Around 30 markers, each trended, with dose changes marked on the chart and every panel tagged trough, mid-cycle or near-peak.
Site rotation and inventoryA body map that suggests the site you've left alone longest, and a count that warns you before you run dry.
Something to hand your clinicianOne PDF: current protocol, adherence, every panel with draw timing, full dose-change history.

What it is not

Protocol does not recommend compounds, doses or protocols, and it ships none of its own. It records what you enter and does the arithmetic you ask for. It is not a substitute for a clinician — particularly for anything involving hormones or prescription medication.
